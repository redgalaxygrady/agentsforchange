# Board (landing) and Brief

Scope: the landing board (src/BoardStage.tsx) is Persuade; the organisation Brief (src/StartStage.tsx) and the rest of the app are Operate and inherit the world with restraint.
Audience: future builders, 16 to 24, any discipline, mostly on phones in a community space with a facilitator. Job: pick a group and one of its problems to solve, then get a brief to take to an AI tool.
Constraints: organisations are made-up examples and say so; no invented claims; NZ English; one built-out organisation (Harbourside, Raewyn) plus bring your own. Org logos stay flat brand marks.
Pinned by Alex (2026-11-14): "human touch made, e.g. clay, craft style". The clay build shipped first; Alex then asked for three directions and picked the field notebook ("Field notebook is good"), with Open Doodles in place of hand-drawn SVG pictures ("Fine for now"). The board is "problems to solve", not "jobs going".

## Direction contract

THESIS: A field notebook someone carries to a community group. Ink drawings of people, notes in red pencil, a highlighter for what's chosen, on dotted paper. AI never gets its own glow; people and their problems lead. Refuses the edtech hero-plus-equal-cards template and the techy AI look.
OWN-WORLD: Notebook paper (#f6f1e6) with a 26px dot grid, ink (#1d2b3a), highlighter (#e4f26a), red pencil (#b23c17). Fraunces 600 for titles and 400 italic for quotes, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading, Caveat for short pencil notes. Open Doodles (CC0) pre-rendered in ink and highlighter in public/doodles/.
STORY: Groups near you have problems they'd love a hand with. You meet a person in their words, pick a problem, decide what AI does and what you do, and show your working.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Paper. Left: headline "Shape something good for the people around you." with the last words highlighted, lede, ink button "See the problems to solve". Right: a person reading in a chair (Open Doodles) with four red-pencil notes and arrows: talk to the people it's for first; AI drafts and sorts; I check it and decide; try it with a real person. Below, Raewyn's photo with an offset highlighter shadow, her quote in Fraunces italic and "Work with Harbourside".
FORM: Notebook entries, not cards: each group is a drawing, a name, a quote and a ruled list of problems. Working pages are white panels laid on the notebook. The record is the notebook written up: ink masthead, highlighter band, Fraunces headings.
SIGNATURE: Hovering a problem sweeps a highlighter stroke across it; ready problems carry a filled highlighter dot and "ready to go" in pencil.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with a visual check at 1440, 390 and 320, DESIGN.md, and the Open Doodles credit in studio/README.
