# Board (landing) and Brief

Scope: the landing board (src/BoardStage.tsx) is Persuade; the organisation Brief (src/StartStage.tsx) and the rest of the app are Operate and inherit the world with restraint.
Audience: future builders, 16 to 24, any discipline, mostly on phones in a community space with a facilitator. Job: pick a group and one of its problems to solve, then get a brief to take to an AI tool.
Constraints: organisations are made-up examples and say so; no invented claims; NZ English; one built-out organisation (Harbourside, Raewyn) plus bring your own. Org logos stay flat brand marks; only illustration and brand are clay.
Pinned by Alex (2026-11-14, latest): "human touch made, e.g. clay, craft style". Look and feel, not literal props. The earlier workshop build (pegboard, tape, index cards, wood grain) was rejected as too literal; the board is "problems to solve", not "jobs going".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Everything you see was shaped by hand. Illustration is modelled plasticine with fingerprints and soft daylight; controls are pressed clay you can almost squash. AI never gets its own glow; people and places lead. Refuses the edtech hero-plus-equal-cards template and the techy AI look, and refuses props-as-costume.
OWN-WORLD: Deep potter's-slip teal ground for the landing, cool pale paper (#eef1ec) for working pages, white cut-paper cards with soft lifted shadows. Clay palette: marigold (actions), coral, plum, sky, leaf, white clay. Clay is a real SVG lighting filter (dome, grain, smudge), never flat circles. Caprasimo for headings, a soft chunky serif like a word rolled from clay; Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading.
STORY: Groups near you have problems they'd love a hand with. You meet a person in their words, pick a problem, decide what AI does and what you do, and show your working.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Teal ground. Left: Caprasimo headline "Shape something good for the people around you.", lede, marigold clay button "See the problems to solve". Right: a clay diorama of a small town on a clay island: the six groups as small modelled buildings and things (a food-rescue van with crates, a hall, a footy goal, a reuse-shop chair, a trap on the ridge, a food-bank shelf) and one empty plot for your own group. Below, Raewyn's photo in a thick clay frame with her quote and "Work with Harbourside".
FORM: User-pinned clay and craft world over the roll (assigned candidate 6, a signed record logbook, binds the record's ritual: entries in your own words, "not recorded" when blank). seed:b6e22788f46a2d9f
SIGNATURE: Picking a problem presses its clay button down like a thumbprint into soft clay; town pieces lift when you point at their group.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
