# Board (landing) and Brief

Scope: the landing board (src/BoardStage.tsx) is Persuade; the organisation Brief (src/StartStage.tsx) and the rest of the app (Make & try, Your record, the brief dialog) are Operate and inherit the world with restraint.
Audience: future builders, 16 to 18 in the pilot (product allows up to 24), any discipline, mostly on phones or borrowed laptops in a community space with a facilitator. Parents and whānau, and community groups, read the landing. Job: pick a group and one of its problems, get a brief to take to an AI tool, bring back versions, leave with a record.
Constraints: organisations are made-up examples and say so; no invented claims; NZ English; one built-out organisation (Harbourside, Raewyn) plus bring your own. Org logos stay flat brand marks.
Pinned by Alex (2026-11-15): "Build the platform round this design language", with four SVGs (two "Agents for Change" wordmark-and-tile lockups, an end slide, and a single pink bite tile). This replaces the field-notebook world (Fraunces, highlighter, Open Doodles), which is now an anti-reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The brand artwork, made usable. A charcoal ground, a big plain neo-grotesque wordmark, and a grid of rounded tiles with cut-out shapes. Tiles stand for the pieces of a sprint: people, making and the record. They turn a quarter-turn when you pick something. Refuses the edtech hero-plus-cards template, gradients and any AI glow.
OWN-WORLD: Charcoal #2B2B2B ground, raised charcoal panels, off-white #F1E9E9 text, lime #DBFF00 for what you press and what is chosen, pink #FFB5B5 for people and their words (and soft alerts). Three tile shapes taken from the artwork's own paths: bite (pill with a hole), gear and molecule, each in lime, pink or off-white, in four rotations. Inter Tight for display and the wordmark (regular weight, tight), Inter for reading. Corners follow the tile: a quarter of the height on controls, 24px on panels, rounded squares for photos.
STORY: Groups near you have problems they'd love a hand with. You meet a person in their words, pick a problem, decide what AI does and what you do, try it with someone, and leave with a record of how you worked.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Charcoal. Left: "Shape something good for the people around you." very large in Inter Tight 400, "around you." in lime; lede; lime button "See the problems to solve"; four short how-it-works lines, each with a mini tile. Right: a 4 by 3 tile grid as in the artwork, mixed shapes, colours and rotations. Phones: headline, a single row of tiles, lede, button, the four lines.
FORM: Brand-artwork tile grid as the signature, over standard web controls (buttons, radios, inputs, tabs-like stage nav). Rank 1 of 1, pinned by the user. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
