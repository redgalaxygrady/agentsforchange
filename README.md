# Agents of Change

Young people (we call them future builders) learn to work with AI by solving real problems for community partners. Over a four-week sprint each future builder picks a local group and one of its problems, makes something with AI, tries it with real people, improves it, and leaves with a portfolio record that shows what they decided, what AI did, and what changed.

This repository holds the project workspace a future builder uses during a sprint. It runs entirely in the browser: no accounts, no server and no AI calls of its own.

- **Problems to solve:** the landing page is a board of local groups. Each one introduces its work, a person in their own words and the problems they'd like help with. One group comes built out with its own material and a fact-check kit, and you can bring your own group instead.
- **Brief:** pick one problem, say who you're making it for, and optionally what you might make and what AI will do. Most people decide what to make after talking to the person, so that can wait. It writes an AI brief you can open in ChatGPT or Claude, or paste into any tool.
- **Make & try:** each version you make, with a screenshot or link, who you tried it with and what happened.
- **Your record:** three moments you pick out in your own words, plus everything above, as a single web page you can download or print.

The app lives in [`studio/`](studio/). See [`studio/README.md`](studio/README.md) to run it, and [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) for how to work on it.
